# Brand guidelines

The brand of **groundwork**, a layered knowledge base of plain markdown files for working with AI
coding agents ([groundwork-md/groundwork](https://github.com/groundwork-md/groundwork)). This file
describes the brand as it is built here: two colours, one typeface, a wordmark whose g drops
through a ground line, and the assets derived from them. The values live in `tokens/`; the masters
are in `assets/logo/` (see [`logo.md`](logo.md) for how they are constructed and regenerated).

The assets are **all rights reserved** (`assets/LICENSE.md`). groundwork is an independent project,
not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude and Claude Code are trademarks of Anthropic.

## Idea

What is below grade is the groundwork. The wordmark's g has its tail dropping through a ground
line; the mark is that g with its ground. Nothing else is drawn: no symbol, no accent colour.

## Colour

Two colours and no accent. Ink and paper trade places between a light and a dark ground.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `color.primitive.ink` | `#16171A` | wordmark and mark on a light ground; the ground of the favicon and the social preview; the dark theme's ground |
| `color.primitive.paper` | `#FBFBF8` | the light ground; wordmark and mark on an ink ground |
| `color.primitive.tile` | `#2A2C31` | ink lifted for the avatar tile only, so the round avatar keeps an edge on a dark page |

CSS custom properties and JSON for these are built into `dist/` (`npm run build`): `--color-ground`
and `--color-ink` follow `prefers-color-scheme`, or `data-theme="light"` / `data-theme="dark"` on the
root element.

## Typeface

Space Grotesk (SIL Open Font License 1.1, no Reserved Font Name), outlined to paths in every master.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `font.weight.wordmark` | 600 | the wordmark and the fine mark |
| `font.weight.mark-heavy` | 700 | the heavier drawing of the g, used for the avatar |
| `font.weight.text` | 400 | the tagline and the repository line on the social preview |
| `font.letter-spacing.wordmark` | -0.015em | tracking of the wordmark |

## Rules

- **Two colours.** Ink and paper, plus the tile for the avatar. Do not add an accent or recolour a
  master; the contrast pairs above are the ones that have been checked.
- **Use the right drawing for the size.** The fine mark is for large uses; at small sizes use the
  avatar or the favicon, which are drawn separately rather than scaled.
- **Keep the ground line.** The g and its ground belong together; do not separate them or redraw the tail.
- **Rights.** Unaltered use to refer to the project is fine; anything else needs written permission
  (`assets/LICENSE.md`).
